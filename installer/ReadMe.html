Gumbo Pot

The installer places the selected formats in your Mac's standard audio plugin folders. Your Mac will ask for administrator approval.

AU: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

After installation, restart your DAW and rescan instruments. Insert Gumbo Pot on an instrument track, click DEMO, then press AUDITION or start your DAW transport.

macOS 11 or newer is required. Pro Tools/AAX is not included.

This installer is unsigned and not Apple notarized. Its plugin bundles use ad-hoc signing.
